# Launch the fresh 100M / 10B Beam trajectory

## Objective

Launch the canonical fresh `100m-10b-data-001` trajectory on one Beam RTX5090
through the full 76,294-update plan. Keep training live while a separate Kaggle
job evaluates the remotely durable checkpoint nearest 5B targets.

## Non-goals

- Do not change the GDN-2 hybrid, model geometry, tokenizer, context, optimizer
  block, dataset order, WSD schedule, or seed.
- Do not resume the completed 100M/2B model.
- Do not stop the training session at 5B.
- Do not start a paid Beam dataset producer; the completed VPS-fed dataset is
  already present in HF and the Beam cache Volume.

## Constraints and decisions

- ADR 0070 owns the VPS-fed Beam dataset path.
- ADR 0071 closes the launch gate and makes the 5B evaluation concurrent and
  observational rather than a continuation gate.
- ADR 0072 updates the Beam client pin required by the live gateway.
- The controlling checkout must be clean and committed before launch.
- Live HF checkpoint retention is rolling latest-only at 500-update cadence.
  The closest durable checkpoint below 5B is `step-00038000`, representing
  4,980,736,000 consumed targets. Kaggle staging must capture it before the live
  pointer advances to `step-00038500`.
