source commit `42b0376511ba1fc7ceecfbbafbeae2027530fc2d`. Microbatch 4 passed
the live four-update gate and production advanced through at least update 99.

stage, and fresh-container visibility passed before GPU dispatch. Startup

distributed-Volume Triton cache override, and RTX5090 microbatch-8 OOM. Those
were corrected without changing model, data, optimizer-block, schedule, seed,
or the full 76,294-update horizon.
